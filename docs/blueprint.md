# **App Name**: VULNIX 2.0 Cyber Myth Symposium

## Core Features:

- Cinematic Hero Section with 3D Model: An immersive hero section featuring an interactive Three.js 3D scene with a floating golden staff, dark fog, and ember particles, designed with scroll-driven camera movements, object rotation, and parallax effects for a cinematic landing page.
- Dynamic Event Cards Display: Displays detailed information for both technical and non-technical events using animated, theme-aligned cards, complete with descriptions and hover effects.
- Responsive Team Registration System: A dynamic form that generates member input panels (Name, Phone, Email, College, Department) based on selected team size (1-5 members) and automatically assigns a unique 'VULNIX-XXXX' Team ID.
- Registration Data Submission: Handles the submission of team registration data, including the unique Team ID, via a client-side POST request to a placeholder API endpoint (designed for future Google Apps Script integration).
- Payment Processing Panel: Presents a payment section after registration, displaying a UPI QR code placeholder and collecting a transaction ID, which is then submitted via a separate POST request alongside the Team ID.
- AI-Powered Mystical Proverb Generator Tool: An AI tool that generates short, unique, and theme-appropriate mystical proverbs or 'challenge blessings' for registered teams, displayed on the registration confirmation or a personalized team page, enhancing the immersive mythological experience.
- Global Scroll & Hover Animations: Implements smooth parallax scrolling, card hover animations, subtle glow effects on UI components, and continuous background motion for fog and floating embers to create a consistent mystical and dynamic dark fantasy atmosphere throughout the site.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Radiant Gold (#FFC100), used for glowing accents, critical information, and interactive elements, symbolizing divinity and strength in the dark fantasy setting.
- Background color: Deep Black (#000000), providing a stark and immersive dark fantasy backdrop that enhances the luminosity of golden elements and contributes to the mystical atmosphere.
- Accent color: Blood Red (#DF3F0E), used sparingly for high-contrast highlights, warnings, and elements demanding immediate attention, representing power and urgency.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif), chosen for its elegant, high-contrast, and somewhat classical feel, perfect for embodying the luxurious and mythological aesthetic of the symposium title and section headers.
- Body text font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif), selected for its readability and modern warmth, complementing 'Playfair' while ensuring legibility for event descriptions and form fields.
- Stylized, minimalist icons inspired by traditional Chinese mythological symbols or motifs, rendered with a subtle gold glow, reinforcing the unique theme and guiding users through the interface.
- Cinematic and responsive layout emphasizing a dark fantasy theme. Utilizes glowing golden borders for key content sections, distinct background layers for atmospheric fog and ember particle effects, and a fluid design that adapts seamlessly across all devices.
- Smooth scroll-triggered animations for all major elements and sections, including immersive parallax effects for background visuals. Includes dynamic card hover animations, subtle glow effects on interactive UI components, and continuous, ambient background motion for fog and floating ember particles to enhance the mystical ambiance.
